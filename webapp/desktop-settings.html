Jun on the desktop

How to play with her, her colors, and her spots.

Move your mouse over her: the cursor and a soft glow show what a click will do there.

Touch

✋
HeadpatRub back and forth over the top of her head.
✋
Ear rubRub back and forth over one of her ears.
✋
Foot rubRub back and forth over one of her feet.
👆
Thigh rubRub back and forth over her thigh.
🤏
TickleWiggle quickly over her side, or click it.
🤝
Hold her handPress and hold on her hand. Let go to let go.
💬
HugPress and hold on her middle (her torso).
✋
Tail rubRub back and forth over her tail.
✊
Tail grabPress and hold on her tail.

Two hands

🖐️
Leave a hand thereWhile holding or rubbing, tap Shift (or right-click). That hand stays and keeps going, your mouse is free for a second touch. Click the little hand, or Shift / Esc, to lift it.
✨
Two-hand combosHold both her hands. A hug with a headpat. Both ears at once. Her tail and her head.

Combos (one after the other, quickly)

✨
Tail grab, then a headpatShe melts.
✨
Hold her hand, then a hugHearts everywhere.
✨
Ear rub, then a headpatDrowsy and content.
✨
Headpat, then a nose boopA giggle.
✨
Tickle, tickle, then her feetA full tickle attack.
✨
Tail rub, then grab itShe'll pout at you.

There may be more combos to find.

Pokes (one click)

👆
Head, cheek, nose, ear, shoulder, tail, hand, thigh, footEach one gets its own little reaction. Too many pokes and she gets annoyed.

Moving her

✊
Pick her upClick and drag. Let go to drop her on the taskbar, or onto one of her spots.
🤾
Throw herLet go while the mouse is still moving.
👋
Wave backWhen she waves, wiggle your mouse near her.

Talking

💬
ChatClick her torso, legs or anywhere without its own reaction. Double-click to send her back inside.
🖱️
Her menuRight-click her: talk, spots, go back inside, hide.

Touch spots

⭕
See themJun menu > On the desktop > Show touch spots puts a faint outline on every spot.
🎯
Move themJun menu > On the desktop > Adjust touch spots. Drag, scroll to resize, untick a spot to switch it off, Ctrl+Z to undo. She holds still while you work.
✋
Her tailIts spot is the tail itself, wherever it swings. It holds still while your hand is on it.

Click a color wheel to pick. Right-click it to go back to the default.
