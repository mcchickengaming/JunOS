Your own scenes (the scene maker)

Make a scene, test it here, save it: it then plays in the real game wherever its slot comes up, if the one gate lets it show (its rating has to be switched on in Settings, and she has to be fine with it). Nothing with a problem can be saved. Scenes from someone else arrive checked and switched on; your own switches decide what shows.

To share them, or to bring in someone else's: Settings > Saves & updates > Jun profiles (the piece "Your scenes"). A profile's scenes are added to yours, never replacing one, and arrive switched on. Scenes can have 18+ content: your own 18+ setting decides what shows.
